Your questions
answered!

Whether you’re a prospective parent or a current member of our community, this section aims to address inquiries and offer insights.

We offer French enrichment programs for students in Kindergarten to Grade 12, with a focus on early literacy, reading comprehension, speaking, and writing. Our programs are designed to meet the needs of both French Immersion and Francophone students across Canada.

We begin each student’s journey with an informal diagnostic and ongoing progress tracking. Our team adapts lessons based on student strengths, interests, and areas for growth using a science of reading approach, ensuring personalized and effective learning.

Our educators are qualified educators who specialize in French as a first or second language instruction. We use engaging, culturally relevant resources and align our programs with provincial curriculum expectations. We also provide detailed feedback to parents and teachers.

Our enrichment support is virtual, allowing us to reach families across Canada. We also offer in-school partnerships and special group classes upon request in collaboration with school boards or community organizations.

Parents receive regular updates, progress notes, and personalized recommendations. We also provide optional reading assessments and support documents that outline next steps for continued growth at home and in class.

We are based in Southwestern Ontario. Proudly serving London, Oakville, Toronto, Markham, Kingston, Ottawa, and Canada-wide.
